Current working pipeline

  1. Observed telemetry

    Reported positions, speed, candidate SINR and cell load

  2. Temporal bipartite graphs

    Vehicle and cell nodes across ten observations

  3. Trust-weighted messages and GRUs

    Local attack estimates reduce sender message influence

  4. Predictions and guarded control

    Serving-link risk, future load, attack score and target selection

Full research pipeline

SUMO supplies movement. Veins and INET will connect that movement to Simu5G. Network telemetry will feed Python inference, and a simulator adapter will apply and acknowledge handovers.
